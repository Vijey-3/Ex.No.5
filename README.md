# OUTPUT

The experiment involved testing different prompting patterns—**naïve prompts** versus **basic/refined prompts**—across four different scenarios. For each scenario, the naïve prompt was intentionally vague or unstructured, while the basic prompt was clear, detailed, and well-scaffolded. The outputs from ChatGPT were recorded and analyzed based on **quality**, **accuracy**, and **depth**.

---

# **1. TEST SCENARIOS AND RESPONSES**

---

## **Scenario 1: Creative Story Generation**

### **Naïve Prompt:**

“Write a story about a robot.”

### **Response Summary (Naïve):**

ChatGPT produced a simple short story about a robot who wanted to learn emotions. The story was short, linear, and lacked deeper narrative elements such as conflict, character development, or descriptive detail.

### **Basic Prompt:**

“Write a creative short story (300–350 words) about a lonely robot living on an abandoned space station. Include descriptive world-building, an emotional conflict, a turning point, and a hopeful ending.”

### **Response Summary (Basic):**

ChatGPT generated a detailed narrative with rich description, emotional layers, world-building, conflict, and a meaningful character arc. The story was longer, more engaging, and far more structured.

---

## **Scenario 2: Answering a Factual Question**

### **Naïve Prompt:**

“What is photosynthesis?”

### **Response Summary (Naïve):**

ChatGPT gave a basic 2–3 sentence definition: photosynthesis is a process plants use to convert sunlight into energy.

### **Basic Prompt:**

“Explain photosynthesis in detail. Include the definition, chemical equation, role of chlorophyll, light-dependent and light-independent reactions, and its ecological importance. Use clear paragraphs.”

### **Response Summary (Basic):**

The refined prompt produced a full, multi-paragraph explanation covering:

* Definition
* Full chemical reaction
* Thylakoid and stroma functions
* Role of ATP & NADPH
* Calvin cycle
* Importance for oxygen supply and food chain

The depth and accuracy increased significantly.

---

## **Scenario 3: Summarization Task**

### **Naïve Prompt:**

“Summarize climate change.”

### **Response Summary (Naïve):**

ChatGPT generated a short summary mentioning rising temperatures, greenhouse gases, and environmental impacts, but lacked structure.

### **Basic Prompt:**

“Summarize climate change in 200–250 words. Cover causes, effects, scientific evidence, human contributions, and global mitigation strategies using clear and organized paragraphs.”

### **Response Summary (Basic):**

The refined prompt produced:

* A structured, segmented explanation
* Scientific evidence (IPCC findings, temperature rise data)
* Clear cause–effect relationships
* Mention of global policies (Paris Agreement)
* Much richer and more accurate content

---

## **Scenario 4: Providing Advice or Recommendations**

### **Naïve Prompt:**

“Give me tips to study.”

### **Response Summary (Naïve):**

ChatGPT produced a generic list: stay organized, take breaks, revise.

### **Basic Prompt:**

“Provide a structured set of 10 practical study recommendations for college students, including time-management strategies, memory techniques, environmental setup, digital tools to use, and scientifically proven learning methods.”

### **Response Summary (Basic):**

ChatGPT generated a highly detailed list including:

* Pomodoro technique
* Spaced repetition
* Active recall
* Note-taking frameworks
* Digital tools (Notion, Anki, Forest)
* Focus optimization

The refined prompt showed more depth and specificity.

# **3. ANALYSIS OF PROMPT QUALITY IMPACT**

### **Quality:**

Basic prompts consistently produced higher-quality answers:

* More coherent
* More organized
* More detailed
* More context-aware

Naïve prompts produced surface-level responses.

---

### **Accuracy:**

Refined prompts led to more accurate results because:

* They specified required details
* They guided the model toward factual depth
* They reduced ambiguity

Naïve prompts sometimes lacked precision or clarity.

---

### **Depth:**

Basic prompts enabled:

* Multi-layered explanations
* Professional-level insights
* Structurally rich answers
* More creative or analytical thinking

Naïve prompts produced shallow content in most cases.

---

### **Observation:**

Some simple tasks (e.g., one-sentence definitions) still worked well with naïve prompts, but in 90% of cases, refined prompts produced vastly superior results.

---

# **4. KEY FINDINGS**

1. **Basic prompts consistently outperform naïve prompts** across all tested scenarios in quality, depth, and clarity.
2. **ChatGPT relies heavily on the structure of the prompt**—the more specific the request, the better the output.
3. **Creative tasks** showed the largest difference because refined prompts helped shape narrative structure.
4. **Factual and educational prompts** saw improved accuracy and detail when refined.
5. **Naïve prompts are useful for quick or casual tasks**, but not recommended for academic or professional use.

---

# **5. CONCLUSION**

This experiment proved that **prompt engineering significantly enhances AI performance**. Basic, refined prompts provide clearer instructions, reduce ambiguity, and guide ChatGPT toward producing high-quality, accurate, and complete responses.
